How this works (the short version)
  1. A simple BPE-style tokenizer splits your sentence into pieces.
  2. Each piece gets a stable token ID and two different numeric vectors.
  3. Spelling vectors hash character n-grams; meaning vectors use a tiny hand-built word lexicon.
  4. PCA finds three directions that preserve as much variance as possible.
  5. The canvas projects those coordinates with perspective, depth, and a movable camera.
  6. This is a simplified demo, not a production language model.
No tokens were harmed in the making of this website.
